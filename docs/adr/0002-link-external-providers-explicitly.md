# Link external providers explicitly

Central Identity supports open registration with a unique normalized username, email, and password; new email/password identities must verify their email before sign-in. Google and GitHub remain available. Invitation records are retained, but invitations no longer gate registration or are consumed by signup. A first provider login creates a Central Identity, but an account from another provider never joins an existing identity merely because its email matches. Link a second provider from an authenticated Central Identity session, even if its verified email differs; the Central Identity email remains unchanged. Existing identities and sessions remain intact. For an identity without a session, operator-reviewed recovery requires independent proof of control; a matching email alone is insufficient. Relying Parties continue to own their users and apply the separate Identity Link rules in ADR-0001.
