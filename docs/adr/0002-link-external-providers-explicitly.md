# Link external providers explicitly

Open Central Identity enrollment accepts Google and GitHub accounts with verified email addresses instead of invitations and passwords. Pending invitations stop working at cutover; back up their records before removing storage. A first provider login creates a Central Identity, but an account from another provider never joins an existing identity merely because its email matches. Link a second provider from an authenticated Central Identity session, even if its verified email differs; the Central Identity email remains unchanged. Existing password-created sessions remain valid until expiry so their owners can link a provider. For an identity without a session, operator-reviewed recovery requires proof of the old Central credential and control of the new provider account. This adds recovery friction but avoids letting an email claim merge identities across providers. Relying Parties continue to own their users and apply the separate Identity Link rules in ADR-0001.
